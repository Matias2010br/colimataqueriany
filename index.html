03 — HISTORIA

Family owned · Latino-owned

A dream, a leap of faith, and abuela's recipe book.

Years ago our family left Colima, México, carrying two things: the hope of something better, and the recipes that had been handed down at home for generations.

Those recipes became a taqueria on East 187th. Then a second one on Bruckner Blvd — bigger room, full bar, murals on the wall, music loud enough to make it feel like a Saturday on a Tuesday.

Everyone who walks in gets the same thing our family got: a seat, a full plate, and the feeling of being home.

— The Colima family, Bronx NY
